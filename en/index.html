Home Page - Pengfei Su

Pengfei Su is a Postdoctoral Fellow at Great Bay University and the Research Institute of Tsinghua University in Shenzhen (since September 2025). He received his Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from Cardiff University, UK (January 2021 – June 2025), supervised by Yue Zhou, Jianzhong Wu and Meysam Qadrdan. Previously he earned an M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering from Tianjin University and a B.Eng. in Electrical Engineering and Automation from Northeast Electric Power University, and worked as an Electrical Engineer at Sungrow Power Supply Co., Ltd.

His research focuses on the coordinated optimisation of industrial process flexibility and low-carbon power systems, including multi-objective scheduling of steelmaking processes, industrial load flexibility assessment and demand response, hybrid energy storage systems, and multi-energy coordination for net-zero power systems. He leads one sub-project of a UK Engineering and Physical Sciences Research Council (EPSRC) grant and has contributed as a key researcher to three major projects, including the EU Horizon Europe programme and an NSFC–EPSRC international collaboration.

He has authored more than 10 SCI/EI-indexed papers and filed 9 Chinese invention patents, of which 3 have been granted. He serves as a guest editor for special issues of Electronics and Electric Power, and as a reviewer for Applied Energy, Journal of Cleaner Production, Energy and other journals.
